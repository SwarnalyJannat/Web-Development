Frequently Asked Questions

📄 How can I download my event tax invoice?

You can download official receipts directly from the "Recent Activities" table on your Customer Dashboard or via the dedicated invoice view.

🍴 Can I customize catering packages?

Yes! Use our interactive Custom Package Builder to tailor appetizers, main entrees, desserts, and dietary tags to your headcount.

🔒 What happens if my date is booked?

If another event holds the calendar slot, our date-conflict system provides suggested alternative openings or allows you to request a waitlist notification.

🛡️ How do administrators verify caterers?

Administrators inspect kitchen certifications, National ID (NID), and CVs directly in the Admin Verification Portal prior to activation.
